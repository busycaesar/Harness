# Never assume

- **Trace everything.** Every statement or decision traces to the requirements or request, the codebase, or an answer from the user.
- **Ask about gaps.** Gaps and ambiguities are asked about, never filled with "reasonable defaults", "common practice" or "probably". Check the codebase first and ask only what it does not answer.
- **Delegated choices.** If the user explicitly asks you to use your own judgement ("you decide", "your call", "use your judgement"), pick your most recommended choice and tell them what you chose and why. Where decisions are recorded, record it as a Decision.
